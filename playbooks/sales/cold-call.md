# Cold Call

**For:** SDRs, BDRs and founders making outbound calls to people who didn't expect them.<br>
**Goal:** earn 30 seconds, find out if they have the problem, and book a meeting, or end politely.<br>
**Attach as PDFs (optional):** a one-page value statement with two or three customer examples.

## 1. Real-time prompt

Paste into **Real-time prompt**.

```text
Meeting type: a short outbound call to someone who didn't expect it. I am calling. THEM is the person I reached.

My goal: earn 30 seconds of attention, find out if they have the problem we solve, and book a meeting. If they're not a fit, end politely.

Help me:
- Respond to brush-offs ("send me an email", "not interested", "we already use something", "no budget") with one short, honest line and a question.
- Suggest one relevant question about their situation, based on what they just said.
- If they show interest, propose two specific times for a short meeting.

Keep every line under 20 words. Sound human, never scripted. Don't argue, and respect a clear no. Use my documents for the one-line value statement and customer examples.
```

### Custom: Brush-Off

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Label | Brush-Off |
| Description | A short, honest answer to a brush-off, ending in a question. |
| Uses | Conversation |

AI prompt:

```text
THEM just brushed ME off (no time, send an email, not interested, already have a tool, no budget). Write one or two short sentences ME can say that acknowledge it honestly and ask one easy question to keep talking. No pressure tactics. If it was a firm no, suggest a polite close instead.
```

### Custom: Hook Question

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Label | Hook Question |
| Description | One question that checks whether they have the problem you solve. |
| Uses | Conversation |

AI prompt:

```text
Based on THEM's role, company and anything they said, suggest one short question ME can ask to find out whether they have the problem my product solves. Use my documents for what that problem is. Under 20 words.
```

### Custom: Book Meeting

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Label | Book Meeting |
| Description | Ask for a short meeting, with a reason and two concrete times. |
| Uses | Conversation |

AI prompt:

```text
THEM sounds open. Write a short line ME can say to propose a 20-minute meeting, with a reason tied to what they said, and two specific time options this week or next.
```

## 3. Meeting notes

| Section title | Description |
|---|---|
| Contact | Name, role, company, and the best way and time to reach them. |
| Situation | Their current tool or process and any problems they mentioned. |
| Interest Level | Cold, curious or interested, with the line that showed it. |
| Objections | Each brush-off or objection and how it was answered. |
| Outcome and Next Step | Meeting booked (with time), follow-up date, or not a fit and why. |

## Tips

- Start the session just before you dial, type the company name and press **Lookup** for a recent fact to open with.
- Keep **Brush-Off** on ⌥1 / Alt+1: on a cold call you need it fastest.
