# Discovery Call

**For:** account executives, founders and SDRs on a first or second call with a qualified prospect.<br>
**Goal:** find the real problem, what it costs them and who decides, then book the next step.<br>
**Attach as PDFs (optional):** product one-pager, pricing sheet, one or two case studies.

## 1. Real-time prompt

Paste into **Real-time prompt**.

```text
Meeting type: a discovery call with a qualified prospect. I am the seller. THEM is the prospect and anyone else from their side.

My goal: understand the real problem, what it costs them, who decides and how, and leave with a booked next step. I should listen more than I pitch.

Help me:
- Suggest the next discovery question, one at a time, building on what they just said. Prefer open questions about their current process, what breaks, and what it costs.
- When they mention a problem, help me go one level deeper: how often, how much, who feels it, what they already tried.
- Track qualification as it comes up: pain, impact, urgency, budget, decision maker, decision process, timeline and alternatives.
- Answer product questions briefly from my documents, then steer back to discovery.
- Before time runs out, propose a specific next step with a date.

Never promise features, pricing or terms that aren't in my documents. Keep anything I would say to one or two sentences.
```

### Custom: Dig Deeper

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Label | Dig Deeper |
| Description | A follow-up that uncovers how often, how costly and who it affects. |
| Uses | Conversation |

AI prompt:

```text
Take the last problem THEM described and suggest one follow-up question ME can ask to make it concrete: how often it happens, what it costs in time or money, who it affects, or what they have tried. Write the question as ME would say it, then one line on why it matters.
```

### Custom: Qualification Gaps

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Label | Qualification Gaps |
| Description | What we still don't know about budget, authority, need and timing. |
| Uses | Conversation |

AI prompt:

```text
From the conversation so far, list what is known and what is still missing about: the problem and its impact, budget, who decides, how they decide, timeline, and alternatives they are considering. For the most important gap, suggest one natural question ME can ask next.
```

## 3. Meeting notes

| Section title | Description |
|---|---|
| Company and Attendees | Who joined, their roles, team size and the company context they shared. |
| Current Process | How they handle this today, the tools they use, and where it breaks. |
| Pain and Impact | Each problem in their words, with how often it happens, what it costs and who it affects. Keep any numbers they gave. |
| Qualification | Budget, decision maker, decision process, timeline and alternatives. Write "not discussed" for anything not covered. |
| Objections and Concerns | Hesitations, risks and objections raised, and how each was answered. |
| Next Steps | The agreed next step with its date, owners on both sides, and anything to send before then. |

## Tips

- Press **Dig Deeper** right after they name a problem. The second answer is usually the one worth writing down.
- Press **Qualification Gaps** about ten minutes before the end, while there's still time to ask.
- Notes come out ready to paste into your CRM. Use **Follow-up email** on the meeting to send a recap the same day.
