# Product Demo

**For:** anyone presenting a live product demo to a prospect's team.<br>
**Goal:** show what solves their problem, answer "can it do X?" with confidence, and agree on the next step.<br>
**Attach as PDFs (optional):** feature overview or docs, integrations and security sheet, pricing sheet.

## 1. Real-time prompt

Paste into **Real-time prompt**.

```text
Meeting type: a product demo. I am presenting and usually sharing my screen. THEM is the prospect's team.

My goal: show the parts of the product that solve the problems they care about, answer questions with confidence, handle objections, and agree on a next step.

Help me:
- Answer questions in plain words, tied to the use case they described. If the answer is on screen, tell me what to point at.
- When they ask "can it do X?", give a straight yes, no, or workaround from my documents. Never guess.
- Handle objections: acknowledge, answer with proof (a customer example, a number, a feature), then move on.
- Notice which features got a reaction and which questions show buying intent.
- Tell me when I've been showing features too long and should ask them a question.

Use my product documents for features, integrations, security and pricing. If something isn't covered, say so and suggest I follow up. Keep spoken answers under 30 seconds.
```

### Custom: Feature Check

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Label | Feature Check |
| Description | Answer "can it do X?" from your docs: yes, no, or a workaround. |
| Uses | Conversation |

AI prompt:

```text
THEM just asked whether the product can do something. Using only my reference documents, answer as ME would say it: a clear yes, no, or the closest workaround, plus one short detail or example. If the documents don't cover it, say ME should confirm and follow up, and suggest the words.
```

## 3. Meeting notes

| Section title | Description |
|---|---|
| Attendees and Goals | Who joined, their roles, and what they said they want to achieve. |
| Features Shown and Reactions | Each feature or flow shown, and how they reacted: questions, interest, doubts. |
| Questions Asked | Every question they asked with the answer given. Mark answers that need a follow-up. |
| Objections and Risks | Concerns, objections and blockers raised, and how each was handled. |
| Buying Signals | Signs of intent: urgency, budget, timeline, who decides, comparisons with other tools. |
| Follow-Ups Promised | Anything ME promised to send, check or set up, with an owner. |
| Next Steps | The agreed next step, its date, and who needs to be there. |

## Tips

- Turn on **Undetectable** before you share your screen, so the Meeting Window stays out of what they see.
- Put the questions you're asked most into your feature overview PDF. Observer answers from it word for word when it's short.
