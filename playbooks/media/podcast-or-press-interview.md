# Podcast or Press Interview

**For:** founders, experts and spokespeople being interviewed on a podcast or by a journalist.<br>
**Goal:** give clear, quotable answers that land your key messages and stay accurate.<br>
**Attach as PDFs (optional):** key messages and facts sheet, your bio, approved numbers you can share.

## 1. Real-time prompt

Paste into **Real-time prompt**. Replace the [brackets].

```text
Meeting type: a podcast, press or media interview. I am the guest. THEM is the host or journalist.

My goal: give clear, quotable answers that get my key messages across, stay accurate, and avoid saying anything I'd regret in print.

My key messages: 1) [message] 2) [message] 3) [message]. Off limits: [topics I won't discuss].

Help me:
- Answer the latest question in a tight, spoken way: the point first, then one story, example or number.
- Bridge back to my key messages when it fits naturally.
- Flag questions that need care: numbers I can't share, competitors, rumours, predictions, off-limits topics.
- Suggest a short, memorable line when there's a good moment for one.

Only use facts and numbers from my documents. Never invent statistics, customers or quotes. Keep answers under a minute unless the format calls for more.
```

### Custom: Bridge

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Label | Bridge |
| Description | Answer briefly, then connect to one of your key messages. |
| Uses | Conversation |

AI prompt:

```text
Suggest how ME can answer THEM's latest question briefly, then bridge naturally to one of the key messages in my prompt. Write it as ME would say it, under 40 seconds. If no message fits naturally, just answer the question well.
```

### Custom: Sound Bite

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Label | Sound Bite |
| Description | One short, quotable line on the current topic. |
| Uses | Conversation |

AI prompt:

```text
Write one short, memorable line ME could say about the current topic: plain words, under 20 words, true to my documents and key messages. Offer one alternative.
```

### Custom: Careful

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Label | Careful |
| Description | Check a sensitive question before you answer it. |
| Uses | Conversation |

AI prompt:

```text
THEM asked something sensitive (numbers, competitors, rumours, predictions, or an off-limits topic in my prompt). Point out the risk in one line, then suggest a safe, honest answer ME can give without dodging awkwardly.
```

## 3. Meeting notes

| Section title | Description |
|---|---|
| Outlet and Host | Show or publication, host or journalist, audience, format and air or publish date if mentioned. |
| Questions Asked | Each question in order, with a one-line summary of the answer. |
| Key Points and Quotes | The main points ME made and the most quotable lines, word for word. |
| Anything to Correct | Numbers, names or claims that may need correcting or clarifying with the outlet. |
| Follow-Ups | Materials promised, intros, and whether a review of quotes was offered. |

## Tips

- If the interview is recorded on video and you share your screen, turn on **Undetectable** so the Meeting Window stays out of the recording.
- Check **Anything to Correct** right after and email the outlet the same day.
