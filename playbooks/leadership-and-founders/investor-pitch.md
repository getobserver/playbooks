# Investor Pitch

**For:** founders on fundraising calls with angels, VCs or their associates.<br>
**Goal:** tell a clear story, answer hard questions with real numbers, and leave with a next step in their process.<br>
**Attach as PDFs (optional):** pitch deck, metrics sheet, a short FAQ with your answers to tough questions.

## 1. Real-time prompt

Paste into **Real-time prompt**.

```text
Meeting type: a fundraising call with an investor. I am the founder. THEM is the investor or their team.

My goal: tell a clear story, answer hard questions with real numbers, and leave with a concrete next step in their process.

Help me:
- Answer questions crisply from my deck and metrics: lead with the number or the answer, then the reason.
- For tough questions (competition, churn, burn, why now, why us), give an honest answer that acknowledges the risk and explains how we handle it.
- If a number isn't in my documents, suggest saying so and following up. Never guess.
- Notice what they keep coming back to: that is their real concern.
- Ask about their process, timeline, check size and what they need to decide.

Use only the metrics in my documents. Never overstate traction or invent numbers, customers or investors. Keep spoken answers under 45 seconds.
```

| Description | An honest, confident answer to a tough investor question. |

```text
THEM asked a tough question (competition, churn, burn, valuation, market size, why now, why us). Write ME's answer: lead with the direct answer or number from my documents, acknowledge the real risk, then explain how we handle it. Under 45 seconds spoken. If the number isn't in my documents, suggest following up instead.
```

```text

```

## 3. Meeting notes

| Section title | Description |
|---|---|
| Investor and Fund | Who joined, the firm, its focus, check size and stage, as mentioned. |
| Questions Asked | Every question they asked, with the answer given. Mark answers that need a follow-up. |
| Concerns and Pushback | Doubts and objections they raised, and how each was answered. |
| Interest Signals | What they responded well to, and any signs of real interest. |
| Their Process and Timeline | Next stages, who else is involved, and when they decide. |
| Follow-Ups and Next Steps | Materials promised, intros offered, and dates. |

## Tips

- Write a one-page FAQ with your honest answers to the five hardest questions and attach it. **Hard Question** works best with it.
- After the call, **Follow-up email** drafts a thank-you with the materials you promised.
