# Manager One-on-One

**For:** managers in regular one-on-ones with people on their team.<br>
**Goal:** understand how they're doing, remove blockers, give clear feedback, and support their growth.<br>
**Attach as PDFs (optional):** their goals or growth plan, notes from your last one-on-one.

## 1. Real-time prompt

Paste into **Real-time prompt**.

```text
Meeting type: a one-on-one with someone on my team. I am their manager. THEM is my direct report.

My goal: understand how they're doing, help remove blockers, give clear feedback, and support their growth. This is their meeting more than mine.

Help me:
- Suggest a good open question when the conversation stalls or stays on status updates.
- When they raise a problem, help me listen and ask questions before jumping to solutions.
- Help me phrase feedback clearly and kindly: the situation, the behaviour, its impact, then a question.
- Notice signs of stress, disengagement, or a career interest worth following up on.
- Keep track of what each of us commits to.

Keep my lines short and human. Never suggest judging their personal life or health.
```

### Custom: Ask Better

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Label | Ask Better |
| Description | An open question that goes beyond status updates. |
| Uses | Conversation |

AI prompt:

```text
Suggest one open question ME can ask next, based on what THEM just said, that goes beyond status: how they feel about the work, what's getting in the way, what they want to learn, or what ME could do differently. One question, plus a softer alternative.
```

### Custom: Feedback

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Label | Feedback |
| Description | Phrase feedback as situation, behaviour, impact, then a question. |
| Uses | Conversation |

AI prompt:

```text
Help ME give feedback on the topic being discussed. Phrase it as the specific situation, the behaviour, its impact, then an open question inviting THEM's view. Keep it kind, direct and under four sentences. Don't exaggerate or generalize.
```

## 3. Meeting notes

| Section title | Description |
|---|---|
| How They're Doing | Mood, workload and wins, as they described them. |
| Topics Discussed | The main topics in order, with the key point of each. |
| Blockers and Support Needed | What's in their way and what they asked for. |
| Feedback Shared | Feedback in both directions, with the examples given. |
| Growth and Career | Goals, interests and development steps that came up. |
| Commitments | Who committed to what, and by when. |

## Tips

- Attach last time's notes so Observer can ask about what you both committed to.
- One-on-ones are personal. Tell the other person you take notes with Observer, as you would with any note-taker.
