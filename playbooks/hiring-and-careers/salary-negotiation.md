# Salary Negotiation

**For:** anyone negotiating a job offer or a raise for themselves.<br>
**Goal:** get the best fair package while keeping the relationship warm.<br>
**Attach as PDFs (optional):** the written offer, your market salary research, a short list of your results.

## 1. Real-time prompt

Paste into **Real-time prompt**. Replace the [brackets] with your own numbers; they stay private to you.

```text
Meeting type: a salary or job offer negotiation. I am negotiating for myself. THEM is the recruiter, hiring manager or my manager.

My goal: get the best fair package while keeping the relationship positive.

My numbers (never reveal the minimum): target [amount], minimum I'd accept [amount], other priorities [remote days, title, start date].

Help me:
- Answer questions about my expectations without anchoring low, using the market data in my documents.
- When they make an offer, help me respond calmly: thank them, then ask for time or counter with reasons.
- Remember the whole package: base, bonus, equity, sign-on, start date, title, remote work, leave.
- Turn "we can't move on base" into other options.
- Keep track of every number they mention.

Only use numbers from my notes and what they said. Never invent competing offers. Stay warm and confident, never threatening.
```

### Custom: Counter

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Label | Counter |
| Description | A confident, friendly counteroffer with one or two reasons. |
| Uses | Conversation |

AI prompt:

```text
THEM just made or repeated an offer. Write what ME can say: thank them, restate enthusiasm for the role, and counter at the target in my prompt with one or two reasons (market data, experience, scope). Keep it warm and short. Never reveal my minimum.
```

### Custom: Other Levers

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Label | Other Levers |
| Description | What else to ask for when base pay can't move. |
| Uses | Conversation |

AI prompt:

```text
THEM says base pay can't move. Suggest two or three other things ME could ask for that fit the conversation (sign-on bonus, equity, title, an earlier review date, remote days, start date, learning budget, extra leave), and one way to phrase the ask.
```

### Custom: Offer Terms

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Label | Offer Terms |
| Description | Every number and term mentioned so far, to get in writing. |
| Uses | Conversation |

AI prompt:

```text
List every number and term mentioned in this call (base, bonus, equity, sign-on, start date, title, benefits, deadlines), marked as offered by THEM, asked for by ME, or agreed. Flag anything vague that ME should get in writing.
```

## 3. Meeting notes

| Section title | Description |
|---|---|
| Offer Details | Base, bonus, equity, sign-on, benefits, title and start date as offered. |
| What I Asked For | Each request ME made and the reason given. |
| Their Responses and Constraints | How THEM responded, including budget bands or approval limits they mentioned. |
| Agreed Changes | Anything both sides agreed to change. |
| Next Steps and Deadlines | Decision date, written offer, and who does what next. |

## Tips

- Before the call, save current salary ranges for your role and city as a PDF and attach it. During the call, **Lookup** can check a number you hear.
- Press **Offer Terms** at the end and ask for the agreed changes in writing.
