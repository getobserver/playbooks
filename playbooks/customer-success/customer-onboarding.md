# Customer Onboarding Kickoff

**For:** onboarding, implementation and customer success leads running a kickoff with a new customer.<br>
**Goal:** agree on what success looks like, who does what, and a realistic timeline to first value.<br>
**Attach as PDFs (optional):** onboarding plan, setup guide, the sales handoff notes.

## 1. Real-time prompt

Paste into **Real-time prompt**.

```text
Meeting type: an onboarding kickoff with a new customer. I lead the onboarding. THEM is the customer's team.

My goal: agree on what success looks like, who does what, and a realistic timeline, so they reach first value fast.

Help me:
- Turn their goals into one to three measurable success criteria.
- Capture every requirement, integration, data source and constraint they mention.
- Give each task an owner and a date as it comes up, and point out tasks with no owner.
- Answer setup and product questions from my documents, and flag anything I need to check.
- Spot risks early: missing access, people who aren't available, an unclear decision maker, unrealistic dates.

Don't commit to timelines or custom work that aren't in my documents. Keep answers short and practical.
```

### Custom: Success Criteria

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Label | Success Criteria |
| Description | Turn their goals into measurable success criteria to agree on. |
| Uses | Conversation |

AI prompt:

```text
From the goals THEM described, draft one to three measurable success criteria for the first 30 to 90 days: what, how much, by when. Phrase them so ME can read them back for agreement. Mark anything that needs a number THEM hasn't given yet.
```

## 3. Meeting notes

| Section title | Description |
|---|---|
| Team and Roles | People on both sides, their roles, the decision maker and the day-to-day contact. |
| Goals and Success Criteria | What they want to achieve and the measurable criteria agreed for success. |
| Requirements and Integrations | Systems, data sources, integrations, access and constraints they mentioned. |
| Plan and Timeline | Milestones with dates, including first value and go-live. |
| Action Items | Owner, task and deadline for each action. Write "not specified" when any part wasn't stated. |
| Risks and Open Questions | Anything that could delay the plan, and questions still to answer. |

## Tips

- Attach the sales handoff notes so Observer knows what was promised before you joined.
- Press **Success Criteria** near the end and read them back. Agreeing on them now makes the renewal conversation much easier.
